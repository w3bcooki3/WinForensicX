Summary

Do first

    When it happened

    Each time-stamped finding on its ATT&CK stage. Grey bands are incidents. Select a point to open the finding.

    Incidents

    Findings grouped because they share a file, account or address close in time, or are high-severity findings minutes apart. The last column says why they were grouped and which finding is the likely starting point.

    Attack path

    Findings placed on the attack lifecycle, earliest stage first, with the time each stage was first seen.

    Findings

    Each finding answers four questions: what happened, when, how we know, and what to do.

    Indicators

    Addresses, domains, URLs, hashes, files, registry keys and accounts taken from the evidence of attacker-related findings. Extraction is automatic: check each value before blocking it.

    Timeline

    Findings and notable changes in one sequence. All times are UTC.

    The printed copy lists findings only; every change is in the timeline CSV and raw/bundle.json.

    Recent changes

    What changed on the host in the look-back window: registry, files, software, services, tasks, accounts, security settings, executions and connections.

    Left out of the printed copy; see the timeline CSV or raw/bundle.json.

    Evidence of execution and user activity

    Programs that ran or existed (Amcache, ShimCache, Prefetch, BAM, UserAssist, PCA), what users opened (ShellBags, Jump Lists, shortcuts, Run box, searches), browser downloads and Defender's own logs. These records outlive the files they describe. A severity mark means a finding refers to that path.

    Left out of the printed copy; see the CSV or raw/bundle.json.

    MITRE ATT&CK

    Techniques behind the findings, by tactic. The mark shows the most severe finding for each technique.

    What this report could not see

    Collection limits. An empty finding list means nothing matched in what was visible, not that nothing happened.

    Not visible or incomplete

    Suppressed by the allowlist

    Case and custody

    How and when this evidence was collected. Verify the case folder before relying on it.

    Verify this case

    Re-hashes every file against manifest.json and reports anything changed, missing or added.
