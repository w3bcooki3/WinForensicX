WS-APT

Do first

    Incidents

    Findings grouped into incidents: findings that share a file, account or address and happen close together, or high-severity findings within minutes of each other. Each row is one story to investigate; the links say why findings were grouped.

    Attack path

    Findings placed on the attack lifecycle, earliest stage first, with the time each stage was first seen. Read it top to bottom as the intrusion story; select a finding to open it.

    Findings

    Every lead, most severe first. Select a row for why it matters, what to do, the evidence and what else happened within 30 minutes.

    SeverityFindingStageTechniqueFirst seenSeen

    Indicators

    Addresses, domains, URLs, hashes, files, registry keys and accounts extracted from the evidence of attacker-related findings. Extraction is automatic: check each value before blocking it.

    Timeline

    Findings and notable changes in one sequence, shown in your local time (UTC in the CSV).

    Printed copy lists findings only; every change is in the timeline CSV and raw/bundle.json.

    Recent changes

    What changed on the host in the look-back window: registry, files, software, services, tasks, accounts, security settings, executions and connections.

    Left out of the printed copy; see the timeline CSV or raw/bundle.json.

    Evidence of execution and user activity

    Programs that ran or existed (Amcache, ShimCache, Prefetch, BAM, UserAssist, PCA), what users opened (ShellBags, Jump Lists, shortcuts, Run box, searches), browser downloads and Defender's own logs. These records outlive the files they describe. A severity mark means a finding refers to that path.

    Left out of the printed copy; see the CSV or raw/bundle.json.

    MITRE ATT&CK

    Techniques behind the findings, by tactic. The square shows the most severe finding for each technique.

    What this report could not see

    Collection limits. An empty finding list means nothing matched in what was visible, not that nothing happened.

    Suppressed by the allowlist

    Case and custody

    How and when this evidence was collected. Verify the case folder before relying on it.
